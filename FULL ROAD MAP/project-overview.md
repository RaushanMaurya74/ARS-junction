# ARS JUNCTION
## Project Overview

Version: 1.0
Status: Production Specification
Project Type: Multi-Restaurant Food Ordering & Restaurant ERP Platform

---

# Vision

ARS JUNCTION is a modern enterprise-grade food ordering ecosystem that connects customers, restaurants, delivery partners, and administrators on one platform.

The system must support unlimited restaurants, thousands of concurrent users, real-time order processing, inventory management, restaurant billing, delivery tracking, and business analytics.

The platform is designed with modular architecture so every module can evolve independently.

---

# Mission

Build India's fastest and most scalable food ordering platform with integrated Restaurant ERP.
